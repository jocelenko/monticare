Why choose us

Structured, accountable and measurable

In complex and high intensity care, reliability is critical. We work to clear standards, defined processes and service commitments you can measure.

What sets us apart

24 and 48 hour intake

We acknowledge every new referral within 24 hours, and start intake and commencement planning within 48 hours, subject to capacity.

Consistent staffing

We keep the same workers with each participant on a stable roster. Less staff turnover means less risk for people with complex needs.

Documentation and reporting

  • Shift notes completed every service
  • Risk assessment and management
  • Hazard reporting
  • Incident reporting inside NDIS timeframes
  • Escalation steps for clinical or behavioural changes

High intensity competency

Workers assigned to complex participants are:

  • Trained for high intensity supports
  • Assessed for competency before they start
  • Supervised to keep quality and compliance high

Risk-aware service

Each participant’s support is guided by:

  • An individual care plan
  • Risk assessments
  • Clear communication with coordinators, families and allied health teams

Communication you can rely on

  • Timely updates when things change
  • Clear confirmation of roster changes
  • Professional, solution-focused collaboration
  • A monthly report for each participant

From your first call to your first shift

Here is what happens after you get in touch, and the timings we hold ourselves to.

  1. 1

    Today

    You ring, or send us a message

    Tell us who needs support and what is hard right now. You do not need a plan number, a diagnosis or the right words.

  2. 2

    Within 24 hours

    A real person gets back to you

    Someone from our intake team acknowledges the referral and tells you who is handling it. No automated replies.

    If we are not the right fit, this is when we say so. We would rather point you somewhere better than sit on a referral for a fortnight.
  3. 3

    Within 48 hours

    We plan how support will start

    We work out what is needed, which of our workers is the right match, and when they can begin. For complex supports we talk to the nurses, doctors or allied health people already involved, so nothing gets lost.

  4. 4

    First week

    You meet your support worker

    The same person, on a steady roster. Workers supporting participants with high intensity needs are assessed for competency before they are allocated, and supervised after it.

  5. 5

    Ongoing

    You always know how it is going

    Shift notes every visit, a monthly report, and one named contact you can ring. If something changes, you hear it from us first.
