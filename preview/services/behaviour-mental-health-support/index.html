Our services

Behaviour and mental health support

Steady, understanding support for people living with behaviours of concern or mental health conditions, from workers who get to know you.

Ring 0447 334 958 Make a referral
NDIS provider Referrals answered in 24 hours

Support that keeps things steady

Consistency matters most here. We match you with a small team of workers and keep them with you, so you see familiar faces who know your routines and what helps.

If you have a behaviour support plan, we follow it closely and work with your behaviour support practitioner. If you think you need a plan and do not have one, we can talk to your support coordinator about it.

How we can help

  • Following your behaviour support plan, shift by shift
  • Keeping routines predictable and calm
  • Help with daily living when your mental health makes it hard
  • Getting to appointments with your doctor, psychologist or mental health team
  • Building the confidence to get back into the community
  • Recording and reporting incidents properly

How we keep it safe

Your support workers are

  • Matched to you and kept with you
  • Trained in the supports in your plan
  • Supervised and supported themselves

Every service includes

  • Shift notes, written every visit
  • Incident reporting inside NDIS timeframes
  • Clear steps if your behaviour or health changes
