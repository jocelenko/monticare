Supported Independent Living (SIL)

Supported Independent Living (SIL) is help with everyday tasks in your home, so you can live as independently as possible.

Ring 0447 334 958 Make a referral
NDIS provider Referrals answered in 24 hours

What is SIL?

SIL is NDIS funding for people who need a higher level of support at home each day. It often suits people sharing a home with other participants, and it can also be delivered in your own home.

SIL pays for the support, not the house. Your rent and living costs are separate. We work with you, your family and your support coordinator to build a roster around your needs and goals.

SIL support can include

  • Personal care and getting ready for the day
  • Cooking, cleaning and running the household
  • Medication assistance, within each worker’s training
  • Building your daily living skills
  • Getting out into the community
  • Overnight support, where your plan includes it

Living with our support

Your support workers are

  • Consistent, on a steady roster
  • Trained for complex and high intensity needs
  • Respectful of your home and your housemates

Your household gets

  • Clear notes every shift
  • Regular updates for families and coordinators
  • A monthly report on how things are going
