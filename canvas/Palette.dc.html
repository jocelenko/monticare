The draft was already on this ramp
Its ring read #A8D5D3 and its field averaged #E6F5F4 across the gradient — each within two channel steps of Aqua 300 and Aqua 50 here. The hue was right; only the ink weights failed.
One thing to confirm with Webster
Light grey works as surface — page and panel backgrounds. It cannot carry body text: anything lighter than Grey 600 fails AA. Reading it as "light grey pages, dark grey type" keeps the brief and the accessibility target together.
