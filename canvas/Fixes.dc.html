CENTRED ON MEASURED INK
+4 RIGHT, +16 DOWN — DELIBERATE
The red crosshair is the true centre of each circle. In the draft the wordmark sits 53px left of it and the whole block 40px low — on a 1010px circle that is a 5.2% horizontal error, visible at any size.
The rebuild centres the wordmark on its own ink, then balances the stack against its measured centre of mass — a heavy word over a light tracked line reads top-heavy if you centre the box instead. That is why it still sits a touch right of and below the crosshair — 4 units and 16 units, both on purpose.
The draft could not be upscaled. It is a raster export with a soft gradient fill and colour fringing from screen anti-aliasing. Everything here is redrawn as vector.
GEOMETRY
01
Wordmark off-centre. Recentred, plus 4 units right for the round e against the flat M stem.
02
Block sagging. Repositioned against the ink centroid, not the bounding box.
03
Tagline drift. Letterspacing left a trailing gap after the final D, pulling the line off centre. Trimmed.
04
Hairline ring. 1.1% of the diameter — gone below 64px. Now 1.6%, with a solid variant for small sizes.
LEGIBILITY & PRODUCTION
05
Tagline failed contrast at 2.73:1 on the field. Now 4.51:1 there and 5.05:1 on white — clears AA on both. This one matters most.
06
Aqua deepened a step, 3.89:1 → 4.52:1 on white (4.03:1 on the badge's own pale field), so one colour also serves links and buttons.
07
Gradient fill flattened. Gradients break in print, embroidery and single-colour use.
08
Type outlined. The draft was set in an Arial-class grotesque. Redrawn in Public Sans and converted to shapes — no font to license or install.
