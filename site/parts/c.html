From your first call to your first shift

1

Today

You ring, or send us a message

Tell us who needs support and what is hard right now. You do not need a plan number, a diagnosis or the right words. If you are a Support Coordinator, send the referral and we will read it properly.

2

Within 24 hours

A real person gets back to you

Not an automated reply. Someone from our intake team acknowledges the referral and tells you who is handling it.

If we are not the right fit, this is when we say so. We would rather point you somewhere better than sit on a referral for a fortnight.
3

Within 48 hours

We plan how support will start

We work out what is needed, which of our workers is the right match, and when they can begin. For complex supports we talk to the nurses, doctors or allied health people already involved, so nothing gets lost.

4

First week

You meet your support worker

The same person, on a steady roster. For someone with complex needs, a stranger every shift is a risk, so we hold the roster rather than filling it from a pool.

At homePersonal care, meals, medication, keeping the house running
Out and aboutShopping, appointments, social outings, transport
Complex healthPEG feeding, epilepsy, wound and continence care
Building skillsDaily living confidence and independence
5

Ongoing

You always know how it is going

Shift notes every visit, a monthly report, and one named contact you can ring. If something changes, you hear it from us first.

Skip the reading

What we support with

Everyday help, and the hard stuff too

We are a registered NDIS provider specialising in high intensity and complex needs, which means we can keep supporting someone as their needs change.

  • Personal care, showering, dressing and continence support
  • Meals, domestic assistance and household tasks
  • Community access, transport and appointments
  • PEG feeding, epilepsy management, wound and pressure care
  • Diabetes support including monitoring and prescribed insulin
  • Hoist and transfer, and manual handling

About us

Based in Burpengary East, working across three regions

Brisbane, Moreton Bay and the Sunshine Coast. We are small enough that you will know the people supporting you, and structured enough to take participants other providers turn down.

MontiCare is a registered NDIS provider delivering in-home support, domestic assistance, daily living support, community participation and high-intensity complex care. We work alongside families, nurses, allied health teams and support coordinators so that a participant whose needs change does not have to change provider.
