Why choose us

From your first call to your first shift

In complex care, reliability is not a nice extra. Here is what actually happens after you get in touch, and the timings we hold ourselves to.

1

Today

You ring, or send us a message

Tell us who needs support and what is hard right now. You do not need a plan number, a diagnosis or the right words.

2

Within 24 hours

A real person gets back to you

Not an automated reply. Someone from our intake team acknowledges the referral and tells you who is handling it.

If we are not the right fit, this is when we say so. We would rather point you somewhere better than sit on a referral for a fortnight.
3

Within 48 hours

We plan how support will start

We work out what is needed, which of our workers is the right match, and when they can begin. For complex supports we talk to the nurses, doctors or allied health people already involved, so nothing gets lost.

4

First week

You meet your support worker

The same person, on a steady roster. Workers supporting participants with high-intensity needs are assessed for competency before they are allocated, and supervised after it.

5

Ongoing

You always know how it is going

Shift notes every visit, a monthly report, and one named contact you can ring. If something changes, you hear it from us first.
