About us

Structured support, not just staffing

We work with participants, families, nurses, allied health teams and support coordinators to put practical, goal-driven plans in place, then we actually run them.

Alongside complex care we deliver in-home support, domestic assistance, daily living support and community participation, so a participant whose needs change does not have to change provider.

Consistent staffing model

The same faces, on purpose

We hold a stable roster rather than filling shifts from a pool. For a participant with complex needs, an unfamiliar worker is a clinical risk, not an inconvenience.

  • Workers matched to a participant and kept there
  • Competency assessed before allocation to complex supports
  • Supervision maintained after allocation, not just at induction
  • Clear communication pathway to coordinators, families and allied health
